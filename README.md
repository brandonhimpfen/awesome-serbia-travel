## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, visa guidelines, and meteorological tracking.

- [National Tourism Organisation of Serbia (TOB / Serbia.travel)](https://www.serbia.travel/) - Official national travel guide featuring regional destination highlights, cultural itineraries, and festival guides.
- [Ministry of Foreign Affairs - Visas](https://www.mfa.gov.rs/en/citizens/travel-serbia/visa-regime) - Official consular portal for entry visa regulations, passport requirements, and diplomatic missions.
- [RHMZ Weather (Republic Hydrometeorological Service of Serbia)](https://www.hidmet.gov.rs/index_eng.php) - Official national meteorological service providing weather forecasts, severe storm warnings, and climate tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating national railway networks, intercity trains, long-distance buses, and urban public transport.

- [Srbija Voz (Serbian Railways)](https://srbvoz.rs/) - National passenger railway operator managing high-speed "Soko" trains (connecting Belgrade and Novi Sad) and intercity rail lines.
- [BAS (Belgrade Central Bus Station)](https://bas.rs/en) - Major long-distance coach network connecting Belgrade with regional towns and international European destinations.
- [Air Serbia](https://www.airserbia.com/) - National flag carrier operating international flights and regional connections through Belgrade Nikola Tesla Airport (BEG).

## Destinations & Regions

### Belgrade & Central Serbia

- [Belgrade Tourist Organization (Tob.rs)](https://www.tob.rs/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the Belgrade Fortress (Kalemegdan), bohemian Skadarlija, museums, and river confluence nightlife.
- [GSP Belgrade Public Transport](https://www.gsp.co.rs/) - Official transit guide for navigating Belgrade's urban tram, trolleybus, and bus network.

### Novi Sad & Vojvodina

- [Novi Sad Tourism Organization (TONS)](https://www.novisad.travel/) - Official visitor guide for exploring the Petrovaradin Fortress, historic Dunavska street, and Danube river beaches.

### Southern Serbia & Niš

- [Niš Tourism Organization (TON)](https://www.visitnis.rs/) - Official regional portal for exploring Niš Fortress, the Skull Tower, and Roman archaeological heritage at Mediana.

### Western Serbia & Mountain Reserves

- [Tara National Park Official Portal](https://www.tara.np.gov.rs/) - Official administration guide for mountain hiking trails, Banjska Stena viewpoints, and brown bear conservation.
- [Zlatibor Tourism Organization](https://zlatibor.org.rs/) - Regional visitor guide for alpine resorts, outdoor adventure parks, and the Gold Gondola ride.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Serbia's digital nomad residence frameworks.

- [Serbia Digital Nomad & Temporary Residency (Ministarstvo unutrašnjih poslova)](https://mup.gov.rs/) - Official government portal detailing temporary stay permits and tax incentives for remote professionals.
- [ICT Hub Belgrade](https://icthub.rs/) - Leading innovation center, coworking space, and technology community hub located in central Belgrade.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, medical assistance, and local safety rules.

- [General Emergency Hotline (Police 192 / Fire 193 / Ambulance 194)](https://www.mup.gov.rs/) - Direct national emergency dispatch numbers operational across all municipalities.
- [Mountain Rescue Service of Serbia (GSS)](https://gss.rs/) - Volunteer mountain rescue service providing emergency assistance in remote hiking and alpine regions.
